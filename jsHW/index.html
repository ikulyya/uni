Rules

Correct the pipe system to reach from the source to every consumer. the final pipe system must be connected in a tree.

  • Click on pipe to rotate it by 90 degrees clockwise.
  • Blue pipes are connected to the water source.
  • Every pipe must be reachable from the source.
  • Every open side of a pipe must be connected to a matching neighbor.
  • There should be no cycles in the final pipe system.
